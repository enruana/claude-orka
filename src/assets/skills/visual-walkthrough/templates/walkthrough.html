One day in the life of the requests

What happens to a request between the form that sends it and the owner who gets it: thirty-six requests as dots travel from the inbox to the work queue, pass the watermark, line up in a checker that looks up their owner, go through an AI reader one at a time, and the urgent ones travel back to the owner they belong to. Walk it with the arrows; the ⓘ has the rules and the tables.
